03 / Research

From Wikipedia-scale knowledge to intelligent assistants.

01

Scalable knowledge infrastructure

Research on storing, compressing, retrieving, and analysing Wikipedia revision histories at scale, including an improved compression approach and a GPU-oriented library for large-scale data.

94% reported compression achieved with the proposed method
02

Knowledge Data Analysis & Processing

Proposed Knol-ML for collaborative knowledge datasets and developed a Python open-source parsing and analysis library.

03

Factoid rearrangement in Wikipedia

Studied the impact of factoid reorganisation and built a sequential model using sentence similarity to understand crowd involvement.

04

WitPy & Wikipedia talk pages

Developed a parser that creates structured comments from Wikipedia talk pages and explored an open-source analysis library.

05

Scale-free network generation

Proposed a grid-based model for generating scale-free networks and provided a theoretical analysis of the model.
